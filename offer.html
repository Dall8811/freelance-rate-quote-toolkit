Freelance Rate & Quote Toolkit

One experiment under a multi-offer umbrella — add/kill later without rebuilding the site.

Stop guessing your rate. Build a reusable floor price and turn it into a clean client quote — with room for fees, unpaid admin, and a tax buffer you control.

The problem

Many freelancers underprice because quotes ignore non-billable time, payment fees, and taxes — then wonder why busy months still feel broke.

The outcome

  • A rate floor you can defend (take-home goal ÷ real billable hours + buffers)
  • A quote you can send without rebuilding math from scratch
  • Separate tabs for fee and tax planning buffers (to set your rate floor) — not jurisdiction tax rates

What’s inside (planned toolkit)

  • Rate planner — annual/monthly take-home target → suggested hourly/day floor
  • Quote builder — scope lines → subtotal → optional planning buffers → planning total
  • Reserves tab — payment-fee % and tax-buffer % you set yourself (rate-floor planning only; not invoice tax lines)
  • Free web calculator — instant estimate while the Sheet is in early access

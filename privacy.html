Privacy

Effective October 8, 2026.

This site currently does not require an account and does not collect payment information. The free calculator runs in your browser and its inputs are not intentionally transmitted to the site operator.

Analytics: we measure aggregate visits to this site using Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other sites, and does not collect personal information — it records only aggregate page views, visitor counts, and referrer information so we can tell whether this experiment is reaching real visitors.

Third-party hosting providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs as needed to serve the site.

This site is intended for adults using general business-planning resources.
